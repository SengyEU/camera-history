    Ask us anything
    How long do you keep history?

    12 months on every package. There are no shorter or longer retention tiers.

    How often do you capture?

    One capture every hour, around the clock. Every package includes 12 months of hourly captures.

    Which camera feeds work?

    Static JPEG, MJPEG, HLS, RTSP or a custom WebSocket. If the feed is public and reachable, we can usually archive it.

    Can I keep my current live view?

    Yes. The widget adds history next to the live image; it does not replace your existing player.

    What if I need more cameras?

    Upgrade to the next package. Starter covers 3 cameras, Plus 5, Pro 10 and Network 25.

    Can I downgrade?

    Yes, after deleting cameras so the account fits the smaller package. Deleting a camera also deletes its history.

    Do you archive private cameras?

    No. Camera History is built for public cameras only. We do not connect to private or indoor cameras.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Plans are monthly per package; cancel and billing stops at the end of the current period.
